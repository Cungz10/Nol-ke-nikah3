## Brand & Style
This design system crafts an intentional bridge between modern, rigorous SaaS efficiency and the soulful, celebratory nature of Indonesian nuptials. The interface balances high-density utility—budget trackers, rundown timelines, vendor vendor vetting—with warm, reassuring editorial grandeur. 

The emotional signature is calming, organized, and quietly luxurious. Planning an Indonesian wedding involves complex family dynamics, significant multi-tiered budgets, and multi-day ceremonial events (Lamaran, Siraman/Pengajian, Akad/Pemberkatan, Resepsi). The design system relieves anxiety through clear typographic hierarchy, gentle warm stone neutrals, and tactile elevated surfaces rather than sterile corporate gray tones.

The design movement is **Warm Minimalist Editorial**: generous whitespace, high-contrast typography marrying high-fashion serifs with functional geometric numerals, micro-borders with translucent warmth, and pill-shaped badges for cultural and logistic state machines.

## Colors
The palette balances warm ceremonial terracotta and rose gold accents against soft champagne mineral backdrops, anchored by deep slate typography to prevent visual fatigue during long planning sessions.

### Color Tokens & Usage
- **Primary (`#BE4F44`)**: Deep Terracotta Rose. Used for core primary calls to action, active navigation indicators, key metrics, and celebratory highlights.
- **Secondary (`#D97768`)**: Warm Rose Gold. Applied to secondary accents, hover states on interactive terracotta elements, delicate progress bars, and subtle focal backgrounds.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: Emerald Safe. Dedicated to positive financial status, budget savings, confirmed payment logs ("Lunas"), and on-track timeline milestones.
- **Neutral (`#1E232A`)**: Rich Deep Slate. The primary text color, delivering superior legibility and softened contrast compared to pure black. Secondary copy uses `#4B5565` and muted captions use `#6B7280`.
- **Canvas & Surface System**:
  - `bg-canvas`: `#FAFAF7` (Warm Pearl Linen)
  - `surface-card`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Crisp White for structural contrast against the warm canvas)
  - `surface-subtle`: `#F4F2EB` (Champagne Stone for table headers, inactive card states, and subtle grouped containers)
  - `border-subtle`: `#E7E4DC` (Delicate warm hairline border)
- **Functional / Semantic States**:
  - **Overdue / Critical Deadline**: `#EF4444` (Soft Crimson) paired with `#FEF2F2` background.
  - **In-Progress / Pending Review**: `#F59E0B` (Warm Amber) paired with `#FFFBEB` background.
  - **Indonesian Vendor Lifecycle Tokens**:
    - *Riset (Research)*: Neutral slate tint (`#F1F3F5` bg / `#475467` text)
    - *Nego (Negotiating)*: Warm Ochre (`#FEF3C7` bg / `#B45309` text)
    - *Booked (Confirmed)*: Muted Rose (`#FCE7E7` bg / `#BE4F44` text)
    - *DP (Down Payment)*: Soft Indigo (`#EEF2FF` bg / `#4338CA` text)
    - *Lunas (Paid in Full)*: Mint Emerald (`#ECFDF5` bg / `#047857` text)

## Typography
The system employs an expressive dual-font strategy:
- **Playfair Display** serves as the ceremonial voice. Used intentionally for page titles, wedding date counters, section anchors, and couple names. It brings romantic prestige and editorial weight, evoking high-end invitations.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** acts as the high-utility partner. Tailored with open apertures and crisp geometry, it handles complex currency reads (`Rp 150.000.000`), vendor statuses, guest counts, and tabular layouts without aesthetic fatigue.

All financial sums, countdown clocks, and guest numbers should render using tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) in Plus Jakarta Sans to prevent layout jitter across dynamic updates.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout implements a 12-column responsive fluid grid pinned within a maximum container width of `1320px` to maintain focused readability on ultra-wide desktop monitors.

### Breakpoint Structure
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid. Canvas outer margins contract to `1rem`, column gutters reduce to `1rem`. Tables reflow into vertically stacked card items. Navigation docks to a sticky bottom tab bar for thumb-first ergonomics.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `1.5rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Sidebar navigations compress to icon-rail formats.
- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12-column grid. Outer margins scale to `2rem` (`space-xl`), gutters lock at `1.5rem`. Fixed 260px vertical workspace navigation on the left, master content canvas dynamically fluid.

### Spacing Rhythm
Spacing adheres to a strict 4px base scale (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px). Generous vertical padding (`space-xl`) frames major module transitions (e.g., between Budget Breakdown and Timeline Rundown), creating an organized, deliberate rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system avoids harsh, unblended shadows and artificial glass skeuomorphism in favor of **Tonal Layers with Ambient Rose/Warm Umber Shadows**. Surfaces float gracefully on the warm linen canvas.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: `#FAFAF7` background. Base layer for empty app wrappers.
- **Level 1 (Card & Modular Panes)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` background with a subtle border: `1px solid #E7E4DC`. Ambient shadow: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(60, 48, 44, 0.04), 0px 6px 16px -4px rgba(60, 48, 44, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Popovers)**: `#FFFFFF` raised. Ambient shadow: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(60, 48, 44, 0.08), 0px 4px 10px -2px rgba(60, 48, 44, 0.04)`. Border shifts to `#D9CFC7`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Sheet Drawers)**: `#FFFFFF` floating surface. Ambient shadow: `0px 20px 40px -8px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.12), 0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.04)`. Paired with a backdrop dimming overlay of `rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.35)` with an `8px` blur.

## Shapes
The shape philosophy employs a **Rounded Tier-2 baseline** paired with purposeful pill attributes for metadata indicators:
- **Base Cards, Containers, Modals**: `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) corner radii to communicate approachability and warmth while holding architectural strength for structured data.
- **Form Controls & Action Buttons**: `0.5rem` (`rounded-base`) for inputs and utility buttons to keep operational density sharp.
- **Status Tags & Action Badges**: Fully rounded pills (`9999px`) to distinguish categorical metadata (e.g., *Riset*, *Lunas*, *Catering*, *Akad*) from actionable rectangular components.
- **Media & Avatar Frames**: Concentric rounds with `0.75rem` rounding for venue photography; circular (`50%`) frames for partner profile images and vendor avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Terracotta `#BE4F44` background with pure white text, bold `label-lg`, `0.5rem` radius, `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding. Subtle transform on hover: background transitions to `#D97768` with Level 2 ambient shadow.
- **Secondary / Outline**: Clean white or transparent background, hairline `1.5px` border in `#BE4F44`, text `#BE4F44`. On hover, fills with a light tint of terracotta (`#FFF5F3`).
- **Ghost Action**: `#1E232A` text with zero border, activating a soft `#F4F2EB` champagne surface on hover. Used for tertiary actions (e.g., "Batal", "Lihat Nanti").

### Status Chips & Pills
- Rendered in `label-sm` with full pill radius (`9999px`) and compact padding (`0.25rem 0.75rem`).
- Designed explicitly for Indonesian wedding planning phases:
  - **Riset**: Border `1px solid #D0D5DD`, text `#475467`, background `#F8F9FA`.
  - **Nego**: Border `1px solid #FDE68A`, text `#B45309`, background `#FEF3C7`.
  - **Booked**: Border `1px solid #FECDD3`, text `#BE4F44`, background `#FFF1F2`.
  - **DP**: Border `1px solid #C7D2FE`, text `#4338CA`, background `#EEF2FF`.
  - **Lunas**: Border `1px solid #A7F3D0`, text `#065F46`, background `#ECFDF5`.

### Input Fields
- Inputs and dropdowns feature a `1px solid #E7E4DC` border on `#FFFFFF` surfaces with `0.5rem` radius and `0.75rem 1rem` padding. Placeholder copy sits in `#9CA3AF`. Focus states illuminate with a crisp terracotta outline (`2px solid #BE4F44`) and zero ring offset.
- **Currency Inputs (Rupiah)**: Fixed `#F4F2EB` left-prefix badge displaying bold slate text "Rp", followed by tabular-numeric formatting with automatic thousands separation (`.`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Precision `18px x 18px` boxes with `4px` corner radius for checkboxes and full circle for radios. Unchecked states carry a `1.5px solid #D1CDC7` border. Checked states snap to `#BE4F44` background with a crisp white checkmark vector.

### Cards & Module Containers
- Constructed with Level 1 elevation: `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #E7E4DC` perimeter border and `1rem` radius. Header rows inside cards feature an editorial `headline-md` title paired with subtle meta actions (e.g., "Filter Vendor", "Export PDF").

### Specialized Product Components
- **Budget Safety Gauge**: Displays real-time allocation vs. actual spend. Green bar `#10B981` indicates under-budget/safe margins; when allocated amounts exceed threshold, the progress indicator dynamically shifts to `#EF4444` with a warm warning badge.
- **Hari-H Rundown Timeline Node**: Vertical chronometer linking ceremonial events (e.g., *Akad Nikah 08:00*, *Temu Manten 10:30*, *Resepsi Sesi 1 12:00*). Each step features a circular state node, an editorial serif time-header, and assigned Person-In-Charge (PIC) family member badges.
